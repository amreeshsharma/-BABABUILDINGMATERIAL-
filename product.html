"BRICKS" are one of the most important building materials used in construction. They are made from clay or concrete and are known for their durability, strength, and thermal insulation properties. Bricks come in various sizes, colors, and textures, allowing for versatile design options in both residential and commercial buildings. They are commonly used for walls, pavements, and other structural elements due to their ability to withstand weathering and provide a solid foundation.


"CEMENT" is a key ingredient in construction, serving as a binding material that holds together various components such as sand, gravel, and water to form concrete. It is typically made from a mixture of limestone, clay, and other minerals that are heated to high temperatures to create a fine powder. When mixed with water, cement undergoes a chemical reaction called hydration, which causes it to harden and gain strength over time. Cement is widely used in building foundations, walls, floors, and other structural elements due to its durability and versatility.
